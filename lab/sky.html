Maunakea sky

The stars over the summit of Maunakea, where Gemini North and the other observatories are. North is up and east is on the left, like a map you'd hold over your head. The edge of the circle is the horizon.

Stars down to magnitude 4.5, plus the Sun and Moon (no planets yet). Star positions and constellation lines are from d3-celestial. The Hawaiian names are from the Polynesian Voyaging Society.
